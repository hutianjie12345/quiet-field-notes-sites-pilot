01

Learning Engine

Source-linked notes and reviewed memory

Experimental personal project
Purpose
Organize learning material and retrieve information with a route back to its sources.
Problem
Collecting notes does not by itself create reliable knowledge or demonstrate mastery.
Approach
Separate source material, retrieval, and human review. The legacy public documentation describes a Google Drive and locally operated n8n bridge.
Current status
Experimental personal project. Implementation and acceptance are reported separately.
Demonstrated capabilities
The existing public information and policy pages are accessible. A bounded read-only memory-retrieval call was observed during preparation of this website; that observation does not validate every component or workflow.
Limitations
Personal use. No public enrollment, learning-data access, or service-level guarantee. The current end-to-end bridge configuration remains to be verified.
