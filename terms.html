Information and interpretation

Operation and availability

The integration’s operation depends on the owner’s authorization and services. Website availability depends on its hosting provider. No service-level or continuous-availability guarantee is offered. The Sites migration pilot does not establish long-term availability.

Materials and privacy

The owner controls the materials submitted to the learning integration and must have the necessary rights to process them. This website is informational; do not post private material in the public issue tracker.
