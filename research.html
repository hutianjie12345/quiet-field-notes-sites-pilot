04Research themes

People, cognition,
and the tools between.

Quiet Field Notes brings research questions into conversation with practical AI engineering. These are thematic interests, not claims of credentials, publications, or completed studies.

01

Social & personality psychology

Questions about individual differences, social context, and how people interpret one another.

02

Embodied cognition

Questions about the relationships between cognition, bodily activity, and the environment.

03

Ecological psychology

Questions about perception and action in context, including what an environment offers an agent.

04

Human-centered AI

Questions about how AI systems can serve human goals while preserving judgment, accountability, and meaningful control.

05

Human–AI interaction

Questions about what people understand, expect, and do when interacting with AI systems.
